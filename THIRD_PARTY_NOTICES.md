# Data and asset notices

## Geographic data

- Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- Source file: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ — public domain. Coordinates rounded to three decimal places for the small-scale globe.
- Airport coordinates: https://ourairports.com/data/ — public domain. Download source: https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/airports.csv
- 76 selected airports; city display names localized in Chinese. Retrieved 2026-09-15.
- Geographic data is for a personal travel journal, not navigation or flight operations.

## Photo

`dist/assets/window.webp` was created for this project using OpenAI image generation. It is atmospheric artwork, not a documentary image of a specific aircraft.

Prompt: A cinematic view from a commercial airliner window at blue hour. A silver swept wing with no branding over softly lit clouds, thin warm orange sunrise on the horizon, deep navy upper sky. Refined analog film photograph, realistic textures, landscape 3:2. No frame, text, UI, logo, branding or watermark.
